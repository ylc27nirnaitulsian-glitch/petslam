## 1. Exa Market Signal & Competitor Teardown

We queried Exa for trending discussions and new Steam/web releases in the virtual pet and desktop companion gaming category (`"virtual pet desktop companion game nostalgia Tamagotchi"`):

### Key Market Signals Identified:
1. **Resurgence of Desktop Companions (Steam & Itch.io):**
   - Games like *Desktop Friend* (Sep 2025/2026) and *TomaDog* are trending by tapping into 90s Tamagotchi nostalgia for people sitting at computers all day.
   - Core player demand: *"A small creature that depends on you gently while you work, study, or game."*
   - Critical Limitation: Every single competitor uses **static, pre-made fictional pixel animals** or alien slimes (*Boing Boing Animals*).
2. **The "Emotional Connection Gap":**
   - Players quickly churn from generic pets because they lack genuine personal attachment.
   - In contrast, pet parents already have deep, daily emotional bonds with their real dogs and cats.
3. **The Gameplay Stagnation:**
   - Existing desktop pets only offer passive feeding or poop cleaning. None of them feature real-time action, competitive leaderboards, procedural cross-breeding, or esports-style combat.
