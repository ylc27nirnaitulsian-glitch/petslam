### What is PET SLAM?

PET SLAM is a next-generation web game that bridges real pet ownership with 60 FPS competitive esports fighting, genetic breeding, and desktop companionship.

Here is what you can do inside the platform today:

#### 1. Turn Any Photo into a Playable Game Hero
Drop a photo of your pet into our AI Avatar Studio. In under three seconds, our vision model analyzes their breed, coat markings, eye color, and personality quirks to build a custom procedural fighter card with combat power, attack stats, and a signature move.

#### 2. 60 FPS Browser Battles
No 40GB downloads or graphics card requirements. PET SLAM runs at a locked 60 frames per second directly in your browser. Tag your pets into the arena, execute aerial pounces, raise energy shields, and trigger the screen-shaking PET SLAM Overdrive.

#### 3. Cross-Breed in the Chimera Lab
Take two pets and enter the Breeding Lab. Our genetic engine combines 6-tone color palettes, facial traits, and a 5-tier rarity ladder (Common → Rare → Epic → Legendary → Mythic) to hatch one-of-a-kind Chimera hybrids.

#### 4. Take Them to Work as a Desktop Companion
Export a standalone Chrome Extension bundle with one click. Your pet roams the bottom of your browser while you study or answer emails, repeats your voice with audio pitch-shifting, barks or meows on cue, and reminds you to stay hydrated.
