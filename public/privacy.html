02. Information We Collect

We collect information only when strictly necessary to authenticate your account, save your in-game companion beasts, synchronize multiplayer matches, and provide technical support:

A. Account & Authentication Data

  • Google OAuth: When you choose "Sign in with Google", we receive your Google ID, verified email address, full name, and profile picture provided by Google Identity Services.
  • Email Registration: When creating an account via email, we collect your email address, chosen Trainer Name, and a salted cryptographic password hash. We never store plaintext passwords.
  • Guest / Demo Mode: If you play as a guest, gameplay state is stored exclusively in your local browser storage until you choose to link an account.

B. Gameplay & Multiplayer Telemetry

  • Battle & Companion Data: Battle outcomes, deck configurations, pet levels, trophies, unlockable avatars, and in-game gold/credits.
  • Friends & Lobby Network: Friend requests sent/received, friend tags (e.g. TRAINER#1234), online status, and temporary party lobby codes.
  • Bug Reports & Feedback: Issue descriptions, browser environment metadata, optional user-submitted screenshots, and email addresses submitted through the in-app Bug Reporting tool.

C. Payment & Transaction Metadata

Digital purchases (such as cosmetic battle passes or virtual credit bundles) are processed directly by our Merchant of Record, Dodo Payments. We receive confirmation tokens, order IDs, and entitlement status. PET SLAM never collects, handles, or stores credit card numbers or financial account credentials.

03. How We Process Your Data

We process your personal information strictly for lawful, legitimate purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and authenticate your player profile across devices.
  • To execute real-time matchmaking, friend invitations, and ranked PvP arena clashes.
  • To deliver automated security notifications, transactional verification emails, and password resets via Resend.
  • To investigate and remediate game bugs, server synchronization anomalies, and balance exploits.
  • To prevent malicious behavior, cheating, bot attacks, and denial-of-service abuse.
Zero Data Sale Guarantee
PET SLAM does not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information, email address, or pet photos to advertisers, data brokers, or marketing syndicates.

04. Pet Photos & AI Vision Processing

PET SLAM features a Pet Photo Studio that allows trainers to upload or take a photograph of their dog, cat, or animal companion to generate a custom combat beast card:

  • Ephemeral Analysis: Photographs submitted are securely transmitted over TLS 1.3 to AI vision APIs (including Google Gemini) solely for real-time visual trait extraction (such as detected species, coat coloration, distinct facial features, and archetype estimation).
  • No Public Model Training: Your pet photographs are not used to train public generative foundational models.
  • Image Retention: Uploaded images are converted client-side into avatar canvas graphics. You may remove or replace your pet's avatar at any time in the companion editor.

05. Local Geolocation & Radar Scans

PET SLAM offers an optional location-based Pet Radar feature to discover wild animal companions in your geographic vicinity:

  • Client-Side Only: When you grant browser geolocation permission, coordinates (latitude and longitude) are accessed directly by your client browser to compute local point-of-interest offsets.
  • Never Broadcast: Your real-time GPS coordinates are never broadcast to opponents, displayed on public leaderboards, or stored in multi-tenant databases. Opponents only see your chosen Trainer Tag and battle lineup.
  • Revocable Anytime: You can deny or revoke location access at any time via your browser permissions without impacting core arena battles.

08. Cookies, Tokens & Local Storage

PET SLAM does not use tracking cookies for third-party behavioral advertising. We use browser localStorage and session storage strictly for vital functional mechanisms:

  • petslam_auth_token: Authenticated session token to keep you logged in.
  • petslam_audio_config: Your master, music, and sound effect volume levels.
  • petslam_active_deck: Cached offline team selection to reduce server round-trips.
  • petslam_guest_data: Temporary progression for users who choose to play without signing in.

09. Your Data Rights & Account Deletion

Regardless of your physical jurisdiction (including under GDPR, UK GDPR, and CCPA/CPRA), PET SLAM affords all trainers full sovereignty over their data:

  • Right to Access: You can request a complete machine-readable copy of your profile and gameplay data.
  • Right to Rectification: You can update your trainer username, companion loadout, or email at any time.
  • Right to Erasure (Account Deletion): You have the absolute right to have your account, player records, companion beasts, match history, and email permanently erased from our systems.
How to Request Full Account Deletion
To permanently delete your PET SLAM account and all associated records, email us at nirnaitulsian@34gmail.com with the subject PET SLAM Data Deletion Request from your registered email address. We will verify and process your deletion within 30 days and confirm when all records have been expunged.

10. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Protecting the safety of young players is of paramount importance. PET SLAM is not directed toward children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children without verified parental consent. If we discover that personal data from a child under 13 has been collected inadvertently, we will take immediate measures to delete that data from our production databases.

11. Information Security & Encryption

We employ industry-standard technical and operational safeguards:

  • All browser-to-server traffic is enforced over TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption.
  • Database tables are protected by PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) policies, ensuring players can only access their own private records.
  • Authentication tokens are cryptographically signed JSON Web Tokens (JWT).
  • Server credentials and API keys are strictly secured behind isolated environment variables.
