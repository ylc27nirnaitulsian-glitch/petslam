PET SLAM Official Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Welcome to PET SLAM (https://www.petslam.app). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide the PET SLAM gameplay experience and account authentication:

  • Account & Auth: When you sign in with Google or email, we receive your email address, name, and profile avatar solely for authentication and player profile setup.
  • Pet Photos: Photos uploaded to the Pet Photo Studio are analyzed ephemerally to detect species and breed traits for in-game avatar generation. Photos are not used for public model training.
  • Local Geolocation: Radar proximity scans process your browser geolocation client-side only to place nearby wild companions on your radar map. Your exact GPS coordinates are never broadcast to other users.

2. How We Use Information

Your information is used strictly to maintain your account, save your in-game companion beasts, synchronize real-time PvP matchmaking, and process purchases via our payment partner.

3. Third-Party Integrations

  • Supabase: For secure database hosting and user authentication under industry-standard encryption.
  • Google Identity: For secure OAuth sign-in.
  • Dodo Payments: For digital cosmetic pass processing acting as Merchant of Record.

4. Your Data Rights

You have the right to request deletion of your account and associated in-game data at any time by contacting our support team at support@petslam.app.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@petslam.app.
