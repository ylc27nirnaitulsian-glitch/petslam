Esports Integrity

Fair Play & Community Guidelines

Effective Date: November 4, 2026 Platform: petslam.app Official Contact: nirnaitulsian@34gmail.com

At PET SLAM, competitive integrity, mutual respect, and sportsmanship are the foundational pillars of our arena. Every match, whether against a wild companion, in a casual friend party room, or on the ranked PvP ladder, should be decided solely by strategy, skill, and deck mastery.

01. Anti-Cheating & Automation Rules

We enforce zero tolerance against cheats and unauthorized automation:

  • No Bots or Macros: Using third-party bots, auto-clickers, or macro scripts to automate battles, coin farming, or companion leveling is strictly prohibited.
  • No Memory or Speed Modification: Tampering with browser clocks, request timers, animation frames, or WebAssembly execution to manipulate attack speeds will result in an immediate permanent ban.
  • No Network Packet Tampering: Modifying WebSocket packets sent to matchmaking queues or friend lobbies is prohibited.

02. Ranked Ladder & Matchmaking Integrity

  • No Win-Trading: Pre-arranging match outcomes between players to inflate trophy counts or leaderboard ranks is considered match manipulation.
  • No Disconnect Evasion: Intentionally terminating browser tabs or severing internet connectivity to avoid battle losses is tracked and recorded as an automatic forfeit.
  • Multi-Account Abuse: Creating secondary accounts to artificially boost a primary account or manipulate lobby matchmaking is forbidden.

03. Community Standards & Respectful Play

  • Trainer Names & Tags: Trainer display names must not contain hate speech, racial slurs, harassment, sexual vulgarity, or personal insults.
  • Friend Requests & Lobby Invites: Repeatedly spamming friend requests or unwanted room codes to players is considered harassment.

04. Responsible Bug Reporting & Disclosure

If you discover an unexpected balance anomaly, infinite resource glitch, or synchronization error:

  • Report the bug immediately via the in-game "Report Bug" button or email nirnaitulsian@34gmail.com.
  • Do not exploit the vulnerability to gain an unfair advantage on the leaderboard or distribute exploit steps to other players.
Enforcement & Appeals: If your account has been sanctioned for a fair play violation and you believe it was in error, you may submit an appeal by emailing nirnaitulsian@34gmail.com with your Trainer Tag and registered email address.
