### Executive Summary

We appreciate the thorough review of our platform by the Dodo Payments risk team. We are submitting this formal appeal to clarify a key misunderstanding regarding our business model and terminology. 

Our application was flagged for:
> *"real-money competitive bidding, live auctions, and paid transactions to secure leaderboard rankings."*

**We wish to clarify unequivocally:**
1. **No Real Money is Ever Wagered or Bid:** PET SLAM does **not** operate a real-money betting, gambling, or auction exchange. The feature colloquially referred to as an "Auction House" was an in-game simulated roleplay market strictly using non-purchasable, earned-in-game gameplay points (berries/kibble). To prevent any future confusion, this feature has been completely renamed to the **"Sanctuary Pet Adoption & Rescue Shelter"** and operates solely via free in-game activity.
2. **Zero Pay-to-Win or Paid Leaderboard Advancements:** All competitive duels, trophies, and leaderboard ranks are 100% skill-based (Fair Play pledge). Real-money purchases provide **0% stat boosts**, 0% damage increases, and zero competitive leaderboard shortcuts.
3. **Primary Commercial Product is Standard Digital Software (MCC 5734 / MCC 5818):** Our primary paid commercial offering is a **downloadable productivity & desktop utility browser extension** (Manifest V3 Chrome Extension) and creative **AI Photo Studio generation passes**.

### Remediation & Immediate Compliance Actions Taken

In response to your feedback, we have audited the entire codebase, live web deployment, and marketing copy:

1. **Purged All Bidding & Auction Terminology:**
   - Removed all references to "real-money bidding", "high-stakes auctions", and "buyout valuations" from our public codebase and API endpoints (`server.ts`, `functions/api/dodo/*`).

3. **Updated Merchant Category:**
   - Our merchant profile is strictly **Computer Software / Digital Goods (SaaS & Browser Extensions)**.
